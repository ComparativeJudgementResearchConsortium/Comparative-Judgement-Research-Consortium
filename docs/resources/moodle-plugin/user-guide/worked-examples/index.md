These illustrated examples are based on the Loughborough University Moodle VLE (LEARN) in 2026. There will be subtle differences in virtual learning environments at different institutions, in terms of how they look, and how they have been customised.

The examples here are taken from a Foundation Maths course where students are introduced to comparative judgement and do formative CJ sessions on 3 different topic areas. Near the end of the course, they are given a question from 1 of these 3 areas again, but as a summative assessment. They do not know in advance, which of the 3 areas they will be tested on. Comparative judgement is used for this final assessment, but by this stage the students are very familiar with how it works and have confidence in the process. We will show the same assignment being presented as both a formative and summative assessment, to illustrate the similarities and differences in the process.
