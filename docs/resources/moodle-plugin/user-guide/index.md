- [Managing the assignment](/resources/moodle-plugin/user-guide/managing/): exemplars, doing comparisons, and managing judges, submissions, comparisons and reminder emails
- [Grading and judgement data](/resources/moodle-plugin/user-guide/grading.html): calculating scores, downloading data and copying grades to the gradebook
- [The student perspective](/resources/moodle-plugin/user-guide/student-view.html): preparing students and what they will see
- [Worked examples](/resources/moodle-plugin/user-guide/worked-examples/): formative and summative examples from a Foundation Maths course
- [Troubleshooting](/resources/moodle-plugin/user-guide/troubleshooting.html)
- [Videos](/resources/moodle-plugin/user-guide/videos.html)

Technical details, including how pairs are selected, are on the [technical information](/resources/moodle-plugin/technical.html) page.
