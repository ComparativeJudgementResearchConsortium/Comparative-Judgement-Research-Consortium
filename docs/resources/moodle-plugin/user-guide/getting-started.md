The Comparative Judgement plugin creates a new CJ submission type to the standard assignment settings with a few CJ specific options that you will need to consider.

When logged into your institutional Moodle VLE, turn editing on by clicking the **Edit** toggle / button. This will usually be in the top right-hand corner.

Click the **Add content** button (usually a plus icon) where you would like the assignment to be placed.

In the menu that opens, choose **Activity or resource** and then select **Assignment**.

This will open the standard **New Assignment** settings where you can give your assignment a name and a description, create some activity instructions and upload additional files if needed.

The **Assignment name** is what students will see on the module page and you can also make the **Description** available there too.

> **Note:** Typically a CJ assignment will be based on an open-ended task where there is no single correct answer.

In the **Activity instructions** tell the students what they need to do regarding creation and submission of items for judging. E.g. “*Explain what a derivative is to someone who hasn't encountered it before. Use diagrams, examples and writing to demonstrate everything you know about derivatives. This should be handwritten on one side of A4 paper, scanned or photographed and uploaded as a .jpeg / .jpg file. Please write in black or blue ink and check that your image file is legible before uploading.*”

> **Note:** We recommend that students judge on a laptop or tablet, however if you expect most students to judge via a mobile phone, then we suggest you instruct them to submit maximum 1 page.

If needed you can upload any reference files / additional information necessary for the task via the **Additional files** subsection.
