This button enables you to look at every comparison made:

- who the judge was
- which items they compared and who they belong to
- which item won in each pair
- how long was spent on the comparison
- the position of the winning item (whether it was presented as Item A or Item B)

This data can be downloaded (e.g. as a .csv or .xlsx file), enabling you, if you wish, to run your own analyses and cross checks of the pairs presented and individual decisions made.
