You can monitor both your uploaded exemplars and the students' submissions, see the table below. You can also Calculate grades and download Raw judgement data, see [Grading and judgement data](/resources/moodle-plugin/user-guide/grading.html) below.

| Column Heading | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Firstname/Lastname** | The name of the student who submitted the item /assignment – or in the case of exemplars, the exemplar title |
| **Submission** | A **View** button that enables you to see the item/assignment submitted |
| **Submission ID** | A unique identifier assigned by the plug-in to help connect submissions with judges. |
| **Comparisons received** | The number of comparisons received by the exemplar or submission. |
| **Time spent** | The total time judges have collectively spent doing comparisons on the exemplar or submission. |
| **Median time spent** | The median time judges have collectively spent doing comparisons on the exemplar or submission. |
| **First/Last comparison** | The date and time of the first comparison and the most recent comparison. |
| **Wins/Losses** | The number of times the exemplar or submission was selected (Win), and the number of times the other, paired exemplar or submission was selected (Loss). |
| **Score** | The score (see [Calculate scores](/resources/moodle-plugin/user-guide/grading.html)), on a scale of 0 to 100, received by the exemplar or submission. This will be blank until **Calculate scores** is clicked. |
| **Include in grading** | Exclude an exemplar or submission from receiving a score. |
