This button becomes available to your judges (usually your students) once the following conditions are met:

- a comparative judgement assignment has been set up and is not currently hidden
- there are at least 2 exemplars or submissions uploaded, not including the judge's own work
- the number of comparisons required (see [Submission types and comparisons](/resources/moodle-plugin/user-guide/settings/submission-types.html)) is greater than 0
- the judge is from a category of users that was given judging rights in the [judging settings](/resources/moodle-plugin/user-guide/settings/judging.html)
- in the judging settings, the start date for comparisons has either been left blank, or you are on/past the start date that has been set

Tapping this button enables you to do some judgements, get a feel for how the plugin works, and consider what will be an appropriate number of judgements for your students to do (based partly on the length and complexity of the specific items being judged).

> **Note:** If you cannot see the judging button after exemplars and/or submissions have been uploaded, try selecting all in the **Judges** box and try again.
