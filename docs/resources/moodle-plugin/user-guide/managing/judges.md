You can monitor the quantity and quality of the comparisons completed by each judge. The overview provided enables you to check that judges (for example) didn't just spend a second per pair. If you have a judge who seemingly did not participate in good faith then you have the option to exclude that judge from any grading of the items.

> **Note:** We recommend that you set expectations with your students regarding the responsibility of being a judge. For marked assignments with peer judgement, we tell students we will be checking their judgements and if they have clearly not engaged properly with the process, they will not pass their assignment.

The column headings are as follows.

| Column Heading | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Firstname/Lastname** | The name of the judge. |
| **Judge ID** | A unique identifier assigned by the plug-in. |
| **Comparisons made** | The number of comparisons made by the judge. |
| **Time spent** | The total time spent doing comparisons. |
| **Average time spent** | The median time taken for each comparison. If this is very low perhaps the judge is not taking their comparisons seriously, and you should check their Ratio of sides chosen. |
| **Min./Max. time spent** | Time taken on quickest and slowest comparisons. |
| **Ratio of sides chosen** | The number of times Item A and Item B are each chosen. |
| **First/Last comparison** | The date and time of the first comparison and the most recent comparison. |
| **Include in grading** | Uncheck this to exclude that judge's comparisons from contributing to submission scores. |
